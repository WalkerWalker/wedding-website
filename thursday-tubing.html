Thursday Morning, August 13 — River Tubing // 8月13日周四上午 — 利马特河漂流


📍 We leave at 8:20 from our local station, or meet at 9:00 at Zürich HB. We start early to beat the blazing midday sun — in the water and floating by about 10:00. 📍 我们 8:20 从我们家附近的火车站出发，或者 9:00 在苏黎世火车总站（Zürich HB）集合。 特意早点出发，避开中午的暴晒——预计 10:00 下水开始漂。

🛟 A morning float down the Limmat. Count on about 4 hours all in, from pumping up the boards to the finish.

🎒 Pack light! Swimwear, flip-flops, and maybe a few sandwiches (there's a park midway where you can grab snacks and drinks too).

🌊 The start and finish are different spots, so everything you bring rides on the board with you — and may get wet. Keep it minimal.

🦺 We'll bring 2 spare small dry bags and life jackets.

🛟 上午在利马特河漂流。从打气准备到结束，全程大约 4 小时。

🎒 东西少带！泳衣、拖鞋，可以再带几个三明治（中途有个公园也能买到零食和饮料）。

🌊 起点和终点不是同一个地方，所以带的东西都要放到桨板上跟着走，可能会淋湿——尽量精简。

🦺 我们会多准备 2 个小防水包和救生衣。

🚿 Afterwards, everyone heads home to freshen up — then we meet again in the evening at the Theater Spektakel.

🚿 结束后大家各自回去洗个澡——傍晚我们在苏黎世戏剧节（Theater Spektakel）再见。
