Friday Morning, August 14 — Pfannenstiel Hike // 8月14日周五上午 — 爬Pfannenstiel山


📍 We start at 9:00 from Herrliberg-Feldmeilen train station. An easy, family-friendly hike — everyone's welcome. And if hiking in the scorching sun isn't your thing, you're just as welcome to come straight to ours, morning or afternoon. 📍 我们 9:00 从 Herrliberg-Feldmeilen 火车站出发。 轻松、全家皆宜的徒步——欢迎大家一起来。如果不想在大太阳下徒步，也完全欢迎直接来我们家，上午或下午都行。

🥾 We hike up to Pfannenstiel — about 3 hours at an easy, slow pace.

👶 Gentle enough for young kids: partly through forest along a little stream (bring a change if you fancy a splash), partly out in the sun.

🧴 Carry enough water and sunscreen — it's a hot, sunny day.

🥪 At the top we can grab an easy lunch, and/or bring your own sandwiches.

🥾 我们一路爬到 Pfannenstiel——慢慢走大约 3 小时。

👶 路线很平缓，小朋友也能走：一段在林子里沿着小溪走（想玩水的话可以带件换的衣服），一段在太阳下。

🧴 带足水和防晒——这天又热又晒。

🥪 到山顶可以简单吃个午饭，也可以自己带三明治。

🕐 After lunch at the top, plans are open:

🚶 Walk down to the lake and take the bus back, or

🏠 head to your hotel, Airbnb or straight to ours to rest through the hot afternoon —

🔥 then get ready for the BBQ party in the evening!

🕐 山顶午饭后，安排随意：

🚶 可以走下山到湖边，再坐公交回来；

🏠 也可以回酒店、Airbnb，或者直接来我们家，在炎热的下午歇一歇——

🔥 然后准备好晚上的烧烤派对！
