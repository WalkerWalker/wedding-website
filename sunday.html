Sunday–Monday, August 16–17 — Mountain Getaway // 8月16–17日，周日周一 — 山中过夜之旅


🚧 Draft plan — still being worked out! Nothing's booked yet and plenty may change. Have a read and tell us if it sounds like your kind of adventure. 🚧 计划草案——还在完善中！目前尚未预订，很多细节还会变。先看看，如果这样的旅程适合你，告诉我们～

✨ The short version

🗓️ Two days, one night, right after the wedding (Sun–Mon, Aug 16–17).

🚠 Up to Stoos — a car-free village reached by the world's steepest funicular — for the famous Klingenstock–Fronalpstock ridge.

🥾 Build your own adventure: hike as much or as little as you like — chairlifts do the rest — and we all meet at the top.

🛌 Day trip, or stay overnight in a mountain hut and head down Monday.

👉 For now, just tell us if you're keen. Times & prices to follow.

✨ 简短版

🗓️ 婚礼后两天一晚（周日至周一，8月16–17日）。

🚠 登上 Stoos——一座无车山村，由世界最陡的缆索铁路抵达——去走著名的 Klingenstock–Fronalpstock 山脊。

🥾 自定你的冒险：想走多少走多少——其余交给缆椅——大家都会在山顶会合。

🛌 一日游，或过夜住山中小屋，周一再下山。

👉 现在只想知道你有没有兴趣。时间和票价之后补充。

🧭 Your day, your pace

The day has three legs — and for each, you can walk or ride:

1️⃣ ⛰️ Up to the first peak (Klingenstock) — chairlift, or hike up.

2️⃣ 🥾 The ridge across to the second peak (Fronalpstock) — the famous ~2-hour ridge walk, and the real highlight.

3️⃣ ⛰️ Down from the peak — chairlift, or hike down.

Pick your level — and we all meet up at the top (Fronalpstock's panoramic terrace):

😌 Easy — ride up, soak in the views, ride down. No hiking.

🥾 Highlight — ride up, walk the ridge, ride down (~2 hrs on foot).

💪 Full — hike every leg.

🧭 你的节奏，你做主

这一天分成三段，每一段都可以徒步或坐缆车：

1️⃣ ⛰️ 上到第一座山峰（Klingenstock）——坐缆椅，或徒步上去。

2️⃣ 🥾 山脊步道通往第二座山峰（Fronalpstock）——著名的约2小时山脊徒步，也是全程的亮点。

3️⃣ ⛰️ 从山峰下来——坐缆椅，或徒步下山。

选择你的强度——大家都会在山顶会合（Fronalpstock 的全景平台）：

😌 轻松——坐缆车上，赏景，再坐缆车下。不用徒步。

🥾 精华——坐缆车上，走山脊，再坐缆车下（步行约2小时）。

💪 全程——每一段都徒步。
🚞 Getting there · Zurich → Stoos 🚞 出发 · 苏黎世 → Stoos

🚆 Route: train Zürich HB → Arth-Goldau, then bus 501 to "Schwyz, Stoosbahn", then the Stoosbahn — the world's steepest funicular — up to Stoos (car-free, 1,300 m).

🕗 When to leave: catch roughly the 08:33 from Zürich HB and you're in Stoos by about 10:15 — around 1¾ hours in total.

🚗 By car it's a similar time to the valley station; we'll confirm the exact train closer to the day.

🚆 路线：火车 Zürich HB → Arth-Goldau，再转 501 路巴士到 “Schwyz, Stoosbahn”，然后乘 Stoosbahn——世界最陡的缆索铁路——上到 Stoos（无车，海拔1300米）。

🕗 几点出发：大约乘 08:33 从 Zürich HB 出发，约 10:15 抵达 Stoos——全程约1小时45分。

🚗 开车到山谷站时间相近；具体车次临近再确认。
🥾 About the ridge · Klingenstock ↔ Fronalpstock 🥾 关于山脊 · Klingenstock ↔ Fronalpstock

📏 About 4.4 km, ~2 hours, rolling gently up and down along the crest.

🏔️ Views over 10 lakes and a sea of Alpine peaks — one of Switzerland's most beautiful ridge walks.

⚠️ It's a mountain trail (moderate): good shoes and a head for heights help. The path is secured with chains where needed.

📏 约 4.4公里，2小时，沿山脊起起伏伏。

🏔️ 可俯瞰 10个湖泊 和层层叠叠的阿尔卑斯山峰——瑞士最美的山脊步道之一。

⚠️ 属山地步道（中等强度）：建议穿好鞋、不太恐高。需要的地方设有铁链保护。
🛖 Stay overnight & Monday 🛖 过夜 & 周一

🛖 Anyone who wants can stay the night at the mountain hut (Stoos Hütte) — cosy, simple and right in the middle of the scenery.

🎒 Pack light — you're sleeping indoors: a small overnight bag, a warm layer for the evening, and good shoes. Days up top are usually mild.

🌅 Monday we take it easy — walk down to the valley, or ride the funicular down. Day-trippers just head home on Sunday.

🛖 想过夜的人可以住在山中小屋（Stoos Hütte）——温馨、简单，就在美景之中。

🎒 行李从简——睡在室内：一个小过夜包、一件晚上保暖的外套，加一双好鞋。山上白天通常挺暖和。

🌅 周一轻松点——徒步下到山谷，或坐缆索铁路下山。一日游的人周日就回家。
🎟️ Tickets & costs 🎟️ 票价说明

🎫 If you have a Swiss travelcard (Swiss Travel Pass, GA or Half-Fare), most of the journey is covered.

🥾 Walk the legs yourself and it costs next to nothing — only the summit chairlifts add a small ticket.

💛 Don't worry about working it out — tell us what you have (or don't), and we'll sort the best-value ticket with each of you.

🎫 如果你有瑞士交通卡（瑞士旅行通票、GA 或半价卡），行程的大部分都已涵盖。

🥾 自己徒步几乎不花钱——只有山顶的缆椅需要一张小额车票。

💛 不用自己研究——告诉我们你有什么卡（或没有），我们会帮每个人搞定最划算的票。
