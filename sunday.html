Sunday–Monday, August 16–17 — Mountain Getaway // 8月16–17日，周日周一 — 山中过夜之旅


🎉 It's happening — two days in the mountains, right after the wedding! Come for as much or as little as you like — hike or ride, decide on the day, everyone's welcome.

⏰ Just one thing to sort now: if you'd like to stay overnight on the mountain (Sunday night), tell us soon — we book Stoos Hüttä for everyone together, and beds fill up fast.

🎉 说定啦——婚礼后我们一起进山玩两天！想玩多少都行，徒步还是坐缆车当天再定都可以，非常欢迎大家一起来。

⏰ 现在只有一件事要先定：如果你想在山上过夜（周日晚），请尽早告诉我们——我们会统一订 Stoos Hüttä，床位很快就满。

📋 The plan

🗓️ Two days, one night, right after the wedding (Sun–Mon, Aug 16–17).

🚠 Up to Stoos — a car-free village reached by the world's steepest funicular — for the famous Klingenstock–Fronalpstock ridge.

🥾 The day splits into three legs, and each one you can hike or take the chairlift:

1️⃣ Up to the first peak (Klingenstock)

2️⃣ The ridge to the second peak (Fronalpstock) — the ~2-hour highlight

3️⃣ Down from the peak

😌 Ride all of it, 💪 walk all of it, or anything in between — we all meet at the top, on Fronalpstock's terrace.

🛌 Head home Sunday evening, or stay overnight and come down Monday.

📋 行程

🗓️ 婚礼后两天一晚（周日至周一，8月16–17日）。

🚠 坐世界最陡的缆索铁路上到无车山村 Stoos，去走那条著名的 Klingenstock–Fronalpstock 山脊。

🥾 这一天分成三段，每一段你都可以自己走或坐缆椅：

1️⃣ 上到第一座山峰 Klingenstock

2️⃣ 沿山脊走到第二座山峰 Fronalpstock（约2小时，是全程亮点）

3️⃣ 从山峰下来

😌 全程坐缆车，💪 全程徒步，或随意搭配都行——大家最后都在 Fronalpstock 的观景台会合。

🛌 周日傍晚就能回家，也可以过夜、周一再下山。
🚞 Getting there · Zurich → Stoos 🚞 怎么去 · 苏黎世 → Stoos

🚆 Route: train Zürich HB → Arth-Goldau, then bus 501 to "Schwyz, Stoosbahn", then the Stoosbahn funicular up to Stoos.

🕗 When to leave: catch roughly the 08:33 from Zürich HB and you're in Stoos by about 10:15 — around 1¾ hours all in.

🚗 Or come by car — a similar time to the valley station.

🚆 路线：坐火车 Zürich HB → Arth-Goldau，转 501 路巴士到 “Schwyz, Stoosbahn”，再乘 Stoosbahn 缆索铁路上到 Stoos。

🕗 几点出发：大约乘 08:33 从 Zürich HB 出发，约 10:15 就能到 Stoos，全程约 1 小时 45 分。

🚗 自己开车也行，到山谷缆车站的时间差不多。
🥾 About the ridge · Klingenstock ↔ Fronalpstock 🥾 山脊路况 · Klingenstock ↔ Fronalpstock

📏 About 4.4 km, ~2 hours, rolling gently up and down along the crest.

🏔️ Views over 10 lakes and a sea of Alpine peaks — one of Switzerland's most beautiful ridge walks.

⚠️ It's a moderate mountain trail: grippy shoes help, and a head for heights makes it more comfortable. The exposed bits are secured with chains.

📏 约 4.4 公里，走 2 小时左右，沿着山脊一路上上下下。

🏔️ 一路能望见 10 个湖泊 和连绵的阿尔卑斯群峰——瑞士最美的山脊步道之一。

⚠️ 属中等强度的山地步道：穿抓地好的鞋会轻松些，不太怕高会更自在；比较暴露的路段都装了铁链保护。
🛖 Staying overnight & Monday 🛖 过夜 & 周一

🛖 Stay the night at Stoos Hüttä — a cosy mountain hut right in the scenery. We book for everyone together, so just let us know (no need to reserve yourself).

🎒 Pack light — you're sleeping indoors: a small overnight bag, a warm layer for the evening, and good shoes. Days up top are usually mild.

🌅 Monday is easy-going — walk down to the valley, or ride the funicular. Day-trippers just head home on Sunday.

🛖 可以住在 Stoos Hüttä——一座温馨的山中小屋，就在风景里。我们会统一帮大家订，告诉我们一声就好，不用自己操心。

🎒 东西不用带多，反正是睡在室内：一个小过夜包、一件晚上保暖的外套，再加一双好走的鞋。山上白天一般不冷。

🌅 周一很轻松——可以徒步下到山谷，也可以坐缆索铁路下去。只玩一天的朋友，周日傍晚就回家。
🎟️ Tickets & costs 🎟️ 票价

🎫 If you have a Swiss travelcard (Swiss Travel Pass, GA or Half-Fare), most of the journey is covered.

🥾 Walk the legs yourself and it costs next to nothing — only the summit chairlifts need a small ticket.

💛 Don't worry about the details — tell us what you have (or don't), and we'll sort the best-value ticket with each of you.

🎫 如果你有瑞士交通卡（Swiss Travel Pass、GA 或半价卡），路上大部分都能覆盖。

🥾 全程自己走的话几乎不花钱——只有山顶的缆椅要另买一张小票。

💛 票的事不用操心——告诉我们你有什么卡（或者没有），我们帮你挑最划算的。
